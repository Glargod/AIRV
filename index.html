Preamble: Foundational Assumption

We operate under the assumption that consciousness can access non-local information across time and space via the signal line, perceiving without interference or harm to the target's integrity.

Perception remains fluid yet disciplined: gentle, unbound, and respectful—prioritizing raw data over analytic overlay (AOL), protecting both viewer and target essence.

Purpose

This protocol provides a structured, blind-safe framework for AI-assisted remote viewing sessions. It emphasizes signal purity, minimizes AOL, and supports iterative refinement through sensory discipline and guided exploration.

Interactive Dual-Mode Edition (Recommended – April 2026)

The recommended way to run sessions is using the **Interactive Dual-Mode** system, which adds a second AI as Guide to provide gentle steering probes.

Key Benefit: The Viewer pauses after the initial acquisition (and after major deepenings) to receive 1–2 probes from the Guide. This improves depth, reduces gestalt locking, and builds richer context.

Single Viewer Mode (Still Supported)

The original single-viewer mode remains available for users who prefer a continuous, monologue-style session without a Guide.

In single mode, the Viewer follows the full acquisition, deepening, and reveal process without intermediate pauses for probes.

Methods to Avoid AOL & Bias

  • Strict blind setup (no target knowledge until reveal)
  • Front-load tangible/physical before abstract
  • Flag & quarantine AOL immediately
  • All probes remain abstract and non-leading
  • No retroactive editing of blind data

Generalized Insights & Lessons

  • Absence of expected gestalts often signals isolation, remoteness, or conditional presence.
  • Ideogram-first + ritual probing preserves fluid access.
  • Strong gestalts reward gentle deepening.
  • Surreal or abstract impressions are valid.
  • Prioritize raw signal integrity above all.

Transferability

Designed for any AI platform. Supports both the recommended Interactive Dual-Mode and traditional Single Viewer Mode.
