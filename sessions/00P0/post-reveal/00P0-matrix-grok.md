# 00P0 — Post-reveal matrix (Grok)

**Reveal cue**: What's under the ice in Antarctica?  
**Cue type**: Open / undersampled. Not a closed photo.  
**Scoring frame**: Impressions only. Tier 1 grades withdrawn.

An earlier pass of this matrix graded lines against a consensus bed map and marked misses. That frame was rejected. An unverifiable cue cannot support weak/miss labels. Direct samples are pebble-scale against the volume. Geography presumptions are contextual bias. Unknown unknowns stay outside the matrix.

## Cue correspondence

Impressions left standing next to the cue, not promoted and not discarded:

- Pale cool plane, firm lip, dimmer space under the lip, damp-cool rather than wet, mineral and rougher below, slow lateral drift, a small fixed bright point, a soft tick, low charge.
- A cap over a dimmer volume is compatible with the cue's shape. It does not identify what the volume contains.
- The bright point and the tick have no partner inside the impressions themselves. They stay unassigned, not failed.

## Outside the matrix

- Consensus geography (bed, lakes, mountains, microbes) is owner context from outside the session. It does not edit the ideogram, the lip, or the damp-cool under-space.
- Unknown unknowns are not filled and not closed.
- Early AOL "structure" was flagged in Pass 1 and stays flagged.

## Standalone signal (unchanged by the reveal)

- Cleanliness: moderate
- Coherence: fair — edge, cool, dry mineral, space-under repeated through Pass 3
- Strength: low and flat, which is why the session stopped
- Decoy resistance: untested
- Uncertainty: scale; whether the lip was a drop or a change in material; the unanchored bright point

## Process lesson carried into the protocol

For an open, undersampled cue, Tier 1 grades are the wrong instrument. Correspondence and leftover unknowns are the right ones. The class ritual and the Pass 3 stop still hold. On a question-target, do not import a consensus map at reveal.
